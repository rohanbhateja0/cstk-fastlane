## 🎯 Project Management Workflow

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Document functional and design requirements
2. **Component Assessment**: Map requirements to FastLane component library
3. **Decision Making**: Determine reuse vs. custom development approach
4. **Resource Planning**: Estimate effort and timeline based on component decisions
5. **Risk Management**: Identify and mitigate project risks early
6. **Stakeholder Communication**: Keep teams aligned on component strategy

## 🚦 Decision Framework

### **When to Use FastLane Components**
- ✅ Requirements align 80%+ with existing components
- ✅ Standard business functionality needed
- ✅ Budget/timeline constraints require efficiency
- ✅ Brand customization achievable through theming

### **When to Build Custom Components**
- ❌ Unique business logic or regulatory requirements
- ❌ Specialized data structures not supported
- ❌ Client insists on completely custom design
- ❌ Long-term strategic component for multiple projects

## 📞 Support & Escalation

### **Technical Assessment Process**
1. **Submit Request**: Document unmet requirements with business justification
2. **Technical Review**: Development team evaluates feasibility and effort
3. **Decision Meeting**: Stakeholders review options and timeline impact
4. **Implementation Planning**: Finalize approach and update project plan

### **Stakeholder Communication**
- **Regular Updates**: Component decision impact on timeline/budget

- **Risk Mitigation**: Document decisions and fallback strategies
